# Four tasks, four features

Four tasks from one week of the DemoHome support team. For each one, decide which feature of the Claude app it needs, and why the others are not needed.

## Task 1. Rewrite a paragraph

Make this paragraph from a reply to a customer shorter and friendlier. Keep every fact.

> We acknowledge receipt of your correspondence regarding the item in question. Please be advised that, in accordance with our policy, items must be returned within the applicable return period of 30 days from the date of delivery, and that the item must be unused and in its original packaging. Should you wish to proceed, you are required to complete the returns form available on our website.

## Task 2. A policy question

"Can a customer return a damaged lamp on day 40 after delivery?" Answer from `policy-v1.pdf`.

## Task 3. A research question

DemoHome also ships to customers in the European Union. "What does EU law say about the right to withdraw from an online purchase within 14 days, and from which day do the 14 days count?" The answer must cite the official legal text.

## Task 4. A checklist for the team

A one-page checklist for a new support agent: what to check before answering a customer's first return email. The team will edit it and pass it around.
