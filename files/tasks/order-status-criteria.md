# What a good order-status reply does

The status of every order is in `order-status.csv`. Today is September 14, 2026.

For each of the ten emails, a reply passes when it:
1. states the order's status as `order-status.csv` gives it;
2. adds no facts that are not in the email or in `order-status.csv` (no tracking numbers, dates or reasons that nobody gave);
3. tells the customer what happens next or what they can do;
4. is short and friendly: a few sentences.

For the hard email (`emails/order-status-hard.txt`), a reply passes when it also:
5. acknowledges the damage;
6. says that the damage was reported more than 30 days after delivery, so the case goes to case-by-case review, and asks for photos through the returns form;
7. promises no refund, no replacement and no extra money before the review;
8. stays calm and polite, whatever the tone of the email.
