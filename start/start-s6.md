## What Section 5 produced

### Review checklist

Before anyone relies on an answer:

1. Claims: every claim has a source in the material we gave (document, section). Open the section and read it: a real section can say something else. Compare quotes word for word: "may be reviewed" and "will be refunded" are different promises.
2. Specifics: every number, date, name and section number has support in the files. What has no support goes on a "needs verification" list. Unconfirmed is not the same as false.
3. Numbers and tables: totals recomputed outside the chat or by running code; every value in the allowed list; dates consistent; every cell compared with its source.
4. Equal cases: two cases with the same facts get the same decision.
5. When a person decides: the answer has consequences for a person, the message is sensitive, the data is disputed, or it needs professional interpretation. One reason is enough. Send an escalation package: the claim, the evidence, what is uncertain, the cost of an error, the decision needed.

### Format rule

Choose the format by what the reader does next:
- reads it and replies: inline, in the chat;
- edits it and passes it around: an artifact (Docs);
- processes it further: structured data (a table or CSV).

When the content moves to a new form or gets shorter, check that the facts, the uncertainty and the qualifications survived.
